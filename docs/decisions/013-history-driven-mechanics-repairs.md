# ADR 013: Repair mechanisms exposed by recorded Earth histories

Seed 2's 30-year trace showed a 60-month German/French war with mobilized forces but zero recorded battle deaths. The old per-front monthly floor discarded every fractional loss. It also emitted 259 occupation records but only seven month-end industrial control transfers: endpoint ownership was read after earlier fronts had already changed it.

Combat now accumulates fractional losses per mobilized country across fronts and months, then deducts whole deaths from the actual adult cohorts and mobilization ledger. Only opposing supplied forces produce combat losses. The scenario coefficient is 0.004 times the existing supply/exposure factor; this is a declared game assumption, not a historically calibrated fatality estimate. No minimum death count or scripted tragedy is imposed.

Occupation claims use a fixed ownership snapshot and the war's actual coalitions. They require an effective attacking force and current numerical advantage. Claims for one city are combined before a single result is committed. Between simultaneous wars, existing oldest-war resource priority is retained, with at most one control change per city per month. Neutral states and allied-owned cities are not converted into enemies by a front's mutable endpoint owner.

The same traces exposed a learn/belong feedback cycle: routine care and research repeatedly replaced life goals. These actions still influence values and confidence, but no longer automatically replace a life goal. Major experiences and real innovations retain their goal effects. A rules fallback now describes its actual short-term purpose while preserving a previously formulated longer-term intention; it does not mislabel a work search as a social or research plan.

Modern Natural Earth regime-specific names are replaced by common geographic labels at creation. Existing active saves append a label-correction fact only for the exact imported source names; alternate simulated names and archived histories are preserved.

Pre-repair long-run traces remain evidence of these defects, not acceptance of the repaired binary. The old Earth 150-year attempt was stopped after its actual 60-year checkpoint for this reason, without claiming a runtime crash or a 150-year pass.

An additional bounds regression reproduced three identity-change entries across twelve actions despite every relevant value already being at its bound. Pressure and change records now use the actual applied delta. A major experience can independently reprioritize a goal when values are already consistent with it; it does not fabricate personality movement. Routine receipt of aid also reinforces values without automatically replacing a life goal.
