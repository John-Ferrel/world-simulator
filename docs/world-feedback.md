# Implemented feedback loops

These links alter typed state and produce causal events; they are not story templates. The simulator calls them inside the canonical monthly transition. Narrative and model proposals cannot independently commit any of these effects.

| Concrete loop                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | Executable systems                                               |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Seasonal production and workforce → grain stocks/consumption → scarcity and price → health, stress, mortality and migration → workforce and next production.                                                                                                                                                     | `world-systems`, `life`, `travel`, `simulation-core`             |
| Wages/taxes → private funds and treasury → paid institutions and dues → production/learning/aid modifiers → later earnings, food and public revenue. Insolvency dissolves institutions rather than granting free effects.                                                                                        | `world-systems`, `dynamics`, `society`, `industry`               |
| Co-location and trust → friendship/partnership → households, births, inheritance and loss → evidenced memory/identity/goal change → new relationships, affiliations and plans. Rumor may change a belief without changing claim truth.                                                                           | `cognition`, `life`, `identity`, `planning`                      |
| Actual jobs, care and organizing → political standing and local network → qualified succession/opposition → paid government transition or failed challenge → laws, legitimacy and future opportunities. Leadership death/incapacity/absence opens a vacancy; ambition alone does not grant office.               | `politics`, `geopolitics`, `planning`, `life`                    |
| Source-backed diplomatic pressure + parties/trade/allies → paid negotiation or military posture → changed dispute → possible war or de-escalation → casualties, supply pressure and government dissatisfaction → future diplomacy and coalition support. The crisis module does not declare war by itself.       | `crises`, `geopolitics`, `industry`, `politics`                  |
| War mobilization → shared national stock and road/rail/sea capacity demand → front-specific supply → combat/occupation/casualties → cohort and personal loss, displacement, legitimacy and fatigue → peace or continued conflict. Multiple wars cannot independently spend the same inventory or route capacity. | `military`, `geopolitics`, `simulation-core`, `identity`         |
| Public organizing/campaign/petition → funded movement meetings and bounded local support → fiscal/legitimacy pressure and qualified autonomy/independence → jurisdiction and tax flows → changed public finance and future movement conditions. Strong empires do not dissolve on an unsupported text proposal.  | `movements`, `state-evolution`, `sovereignty`, `world-systems`   |
| Actual occupation/recapture/autonomy/independence → city control → local industrial capacity transfer and government/diplomacy reconciliation → future production, supply and war fronts. National stocks are retained, not duplicated; a newly neutral region loses obsolete war fronts.                        | `sovereignty`, `industry`, `state-evolution`, `geopolitics`      |
| Cross-month public activity + restrictive laws + administrative capacity → funded custody → paused work/travel/agency, stress/health and kin effects → identity/replanning/unrest/legitimacy → reform, regime change or release. Activities are evidence of public activity, not criminal convictions.           | `coercion`, `life`, `travel`, `planning`, `politics`, `identity` |
| Route capacity, distance, fee, food and restrictions → journey progress or blockage → actual arrival/location → work, relationships and political eligibility → future funds, needs and travel choices. In-transit people do not continue local interactions at their last confirmed origin.                     | `travel`, `life`, `cognition`, `planning`, `politics`            |
| Paid study/research and maintained academies → bounded discovery/skills → production and learning → prosperity/revenue → capacity to maintain future research. Unsupported inventions remain failed ideas/claims.                                                                                                | `dynamics`, `planning`, `industry`, `cognition`                  |

The 1900 sovereignty snapshot initializes only a new world. Later history depends on these loops; real-world dates do not restore a prewritten control map. Source events may be delayed, suppressed or take different consequences as prerequisites change.

Limits: monthly aggregate logistics and cohorts, a staple/currency plus six industrial goods, bounded social/organization grammars, administrative rather than full judicial custody, and no detailed naval/air combat. Civil shipping capacity is not a navy. Movement support tags are normalized scenario participation, not poll data. Modern geographic geometry is not a claim of exact historical borders.
