## 2. Strongest moments

1. **Klara, 1943.07 → 1952.01 — the denunciation comes back.** She writes two pages saying Berta Weiss 2 has strangers at night; Berta is questioned. Nine years later, in the housing office, Klara says “当年那封信是我写的。” Berta asks why; Klara does not move until the clock strikes four. This is a committed act, a named victim, a later institutional encounter, and no cheap absolution. (Existing history content, not a Phase 17 pack.)
2. **Klara, 1957.01 — the borrowed name.** Maria Weiss opens an old register and has Klara sign after “会长.” Three days later: a brass key and a letter of appointment to the three-member night-school alumni association, duties limited to a storage room whose lock is broken. A wish for power is granted in the smallest possible form. (Existing curse pack.)
3. **Josef, 1934.01 — the copied bookcase.** The apprentice takes Josef’s drawing to another workshop; the same bookcase appears in the market with thinner brass and a 30% lower price. Josef takes the final payment, goes back, and locks the original drawing in a cabinet. This is the strongest new-pack scene: a craft object, a betrayal, a price, and a consequence that is not a speech.

5. **Lena, 1931.01 — the alligator wallet.** She finds it at the opera: nine kronen, a standing ticket, a piano tuner’s card. 7. **Mitzi, 1941.04 — the story sold under another name.** She sells an unfinished manuscript to a pulp publisher for one month’s rent. The editor changes the ending to a train crash and puts a stranger’s pen name on the cover. 8. **Klara, 1962.01 — buying the witness.** The family scrapes together three kronen and hands it to Mitzi Weiss behind the café. The next day Mitzi says the light was too dim to identify anyone; the inspector closes the book but not the case. (New `cj17` pack.)

## 3. Weaknesses

### 3.1 The relationship and child layer is a slot machine, not a life

- Josef has at least eight named partners/courtship targets in 15 years; the same “空咖啡杯转了半圈” text appears **six times** with different names. Several relationships begin without the previous one ending.
- Lena gives birth **fourteen times in fourteen consecutive years, 1939–1952**, with duplicate names (Mitzi twice, Gustav twice, Rosa twice, Katharina twice). There is no transition from Mitzi Lechner (moves out 1935) to Friedrich Winter, the father of the 1939–1952 children.
- Klara has seven births between 1931 and 1938; after Hermann Weiss moved out in 1936, a difficult birth with Ferdinand Moser in 1938 has no courtship/partner transition.

This is not “many events”; it is a family generator that does not maintain a family.

### 3.2 New signature states are dead or cameos

In the primary 50-year world:

- **Eight of 24 new states never appear:** `ds17-double-life`, `ds17-kept-secret`, `cj17-prison-term`, `cj17-protection-racket`, `mb17-stateless`, `fa17-plagiarist`, `af17-mistaken-heir`, `af17-cursed-luck`.
- **29 of 79 catalog states are absent from the primary world**, including shared hooks `family-estate`, `war-widow`, `war-widow-with-children`, `known-affair`, `secret-lover`, `debt-bondage`, `university-graduate`, `university-dropout`, `thesis-candidate`.
- **The dead states have authored consumers but unreachable conditions.** `af17-mistaken-heir` needs `wish:wealth` (zero instances); `ds17-double-life` needs `secret_intimacy` (zero in b); `cj17-prison-term` never fires despite six `detained`; `fa17-plagiarist` never fires despite 20 `fa17-starving-artist`; `mb17-stateless` never fires because permit scenes only produce `turned-back`.

The catalog’s “external states consumed” column is not evidence that the chain happened.

### 3.3 Template ticks dominate; repetition increased

- `raising-child`: **1,045 ticks and 683 reactions** in primary b (1,728 events, 8.1% of 21,405). Lena alone has 37 raising-child events, 17 school notes and 14 births. The note text repeats 17 times; the glass still contains “three notes.”
- `wc17-master-craftsperson/workshop-year/commissions-arrive` appears six times for Josef with the same “一扇橡木门、一只修到一半的八音盒”; backlog rises but never opens a decision.
- `fa17-starving-artist/rent-and-pages/another-rejection` repeats three times for Mitzi with the same rejected-painting text.
- `lc17-caregiver/care-year/worn-thin` repeats four times for Rosa with the same fall/hospital/job-loss text.
- `wish-power/power-office` appears seven times for Klara with the same “签下自己的名字…愿望没有因此变近”; it only changes reputation. `wish-power.intensity` never changes.
- Reported repeatRate: **0.1978 in b / 0.1922 in a**, higher than Phase 16’s 0.1763/0.1787. The automated critic still passes with `findings: []`.

### 3.4 State ownership, links and source events are broken

- **Dead people own future households.** Emil dies 1920, but the snapshot shows `raising-child` states from 1931–1953 and `mb17-remittance-web` active in 1959. Mitzi dies 1928 but has `raising-child` states 1939–1943. Rosa dies 1936 but has active `raising-child` states 1958–1961.
- **The caregiver is linked to herself.** In a, Rosa’s `lc17-caregiver` has `links.patient = Rosa Lechner`; the tick says Rosa falls, Rosa sleeps in the corridor, Rosa loses the factory job. The separate crisis scene correctly uses Alois Lechner as patient.
- **Self-cast childhood.** Mitzi b 1914.07: the baby and the caretaker are the same person, and the event adds a kin relation to herself.
- **Empty nest contradicts school notes.** Lena adds `lc17-empty-nest` in 1962.01 while her `raising-child` states still have `leftHome: false`; school notes follow in 1962.07/1962.11.
- **States appear without a visible cause.** All nine b `mb17-remittance-web` snapshots trace to events outside the focal person’s list; the reader sees the obligation but never the scene that created it.

### 3.5 Historical chronology and role gating are unreliable

- Rosa’s liberation flag is dated **1936.12**, nine years before liberation; Klara’s liberation is **1946.01**, a year late; Anschluss is January 1938.
- Anton joins the Wiener Psychoanalytische Vereinigung in 1937.09 from a leaflet and becomes block liaison; in 1954 he joins **Die Fackel** as a political party. These are role/category errors.
- A historical-process title is malformed: “历史进程《权力俱乐部在…把分散的行动整理成公开纲领；这不是预先》从 emerging 进入 mobilizing.”
- Josef’s 1956.05 `workshop-closure/wages-cut` carries **62 historyRefs**, a dump of prior events rather than a causal chain.

### 3.6 The curse still self-casts and still lacks a cross-person consequence

Hedwig 1930.01/1931.01 reactions say Hedwig herself falls ill while Anna Moser 2/Hermann Weiss “go out as usual.” Effects are only `state_parameter cursed-by-wish` and mood; there is no health/condition on the named betrayer. The prose still does the work the mechanics do not. This contradicts the Phase 16 revision claim; it is a real remaining bug.

### 3.7 Half the sample is not a full life, and survivors have no horizon

- Primary b: Emil dies at 8 with 7 events; Mitzi at 16 with 33; Hedwig at 20 with 60; Rosa at 24 with 61. These are deaths, not adult lives.
- Klara, Lena and Anton are alive in 1962 and stop: Klara’s last event is a union ledger, Lena’s a school note, Anton’s a clock repair. There is no horizon/coda and no settlement of the final-year `cj17-wanted`.
- Secondary a has Anton with **0 subject events**, Hedwig 23, Josef stops in 1935 with no death scene.

### 3.8 Organizations and positions duplicate rather than compound

- Klara founds **权力俱乐部** at 1934, 1941 and 1951 — three separate organizations with the same name — then “接手后的俱乐部” in 1956. The world ends with 62 positions: 10 师傅, 9 助理文书, 6 联络员, 5 顾问.
- Josef founds **师傅作坊** at 1935 and again 1946, two organizations with the same name/authority. `office-under-siege` then attaches to the workshop’s 师傅 position as if a craft workshop were a political office.
- The three-member 夜校同学会 becomes a “historical process” in 1959 with a public program.

## 5. Is Phase 17 meaningfully richer and more combinable?

**Mostly a larger library.** It is not a fake expansion — the new packs contain good scenes and real state definitions — but the median life is not meaningfully richer and the combinable core is thin.

Improved:

- Real authored scenes at the top: Josef’s copied bookcase, Lena’s opera wallet, Klara’s borrowed-name appointment, Mitzi’s pulp story.
- A few real cross-pack chains: work → workshop position → office-under-siege; under-investigation → crime → wanted; courtship → forbidden-attachment; curse-bargain → office/history.
- The new packs are generally unsentimental; the problem is not preachiness.

Not improved enough:

- Primary sample still dominated by old family/medical/rent base; new packs 3.1% of focal events; `raising-child` alone 8.1% of the world.
- Eight new states never appear in b; seven in neither run; 29 catalog states absent from b.
- Repetition worse than Phase 16 (19.78% vs 17.63%); automated critic still passes.
- Four of eight primary lives end before 25; survivors stop without horizon; best 20/30 below Phase 16’s 23.
- Organizations/offices duplicate rather than accumulate.

The honest summary: **Phase 16 was a real mechanism occasionally used downstream inside a still-fragmented life simulator. Phase 17 is a larger, better-written library whose best scenes are still isolated, and whose new mechanisms are mostly not on the focal path. It is a content-production success and a conversion failure.**

## 6. Revision priorities

1. **Make the family/relationship layer a chain, not a slot machine.** Add partner/age/birth interval/once-per-person conditions; end or continue partnerships before new ones; one school note per child per stage; stop school notes at `leftHome`.
2. **Revive dead signature states and cross-pack conditions.** Remove impossible `wish:wealth` gates; trigger `ds17-double-life` from existing `forbidden-attachment`/partner; lower court/prison gate; make `fa17-plagiarist` reachable; add origin gate to migration; create missing old hooks.
3. **Replace annual template ticks with staged, consequential beats.** One masterwork per life, then copied design/stolen pattern/debt winter; three stages for starving artist; one fall/crisis/death for caregiver; wish reactions should change intensity/fixation.
4. **Condition history, roles, and endings.** Gate liberation 1945+, Anschluss March 1938, organization recruitment by type, household states by living owner, bind caregiver patient; add a 50-year horizon/coda for every living focal.
5. Do not add a ninth pack until 1–3 are done.

## 7. Revision response (after the critique)

The following changes were made after the independent review; they are a response, not a claim that the score is now 16/30 or better.

### 7.1 Silent no-op fix

- **18 `cash` effects** in `pack-factory-education` (14), `pack-life-course` (2) and `pack-absurd-fortune` (2) used `personId`, which `applyCash` ignores. They now use `householdId: "$household"`. Factory tuition/stipend/debt and the chronic-illness/wallet costs now move household cash.
- Factory pack: `skill: "intelligence"` → `literacy`; invalid `education` delta → `status: "none"`; all outcome texts raised above 40 characters; missing `ageWindow`s added.
- Factory `night-school` now has a graduation transition at `progress >= 60` to `university-graduate`; `graduation-mismatch` no longer gates on `cash max 120`.

### 7.2 State graph / slots

- Added a **player-subject caregiver entry** `lc17-care.offers-to-care` (move the parent in / hire help / postpone), with `links.patient = "$parent"`. The caregiver state is no longer only assigned by the parent’s scene.
- Added `links.artist = "$director"` to the `fa17-muse` state created by the cabaret small-part; the unresolved `{artist}` tick text is gone.
- Gave Phase 17 states non-generic slots: `childhood_talent`, `double_life`, `kept_secret`, `muse_identity`, `false_heir`; `office-under-siege` moved to `office_pressure`, so it no longer evicts `office-patronage`.
- Added a threshold gate: `fa17-press.plagiarism-accusation` now requires `fa17-plagiarist.exposure >= 20`, so the plagiarism parameter can actually gate its crisis.
- Fixed fame stage/age boundaries: cabaret debut min 18; late recognition min 60.

### 7.3 Diagnostics and honesty

- Dossiers no longer show household/organization states for a dead focal person (the “dead people own future households” artifact).
- Added `contentSamples` (new-pack events with text/effects) and `crossPack` counts to the lifetime JSON.
- Added `tests/v4-phase17-content.test.ts`: all generated packs validate, ids are unique, every generated state has a real consumer and a creation path, every generated kernel has ≥2 outcomes, and compiled scenes > 0.
- Catalog now includes pack metadata, consumer packs, stage/domain gaps and unconsumed states.

### 7.5 What remains

The critique still stands in its largest points:

- relationship/child generation is still slot-machine-like;
- dead/rare signature states remain (double-life, mistaken-heir, cursed-luck, prison-term, stateless, thesis-candidate);
- new packs are still mostly on the NPC periphery, not the focal path;
- organizations/positions duplicate;
- historical dates are still placeholders/misaligned;
- survivors still lack an explicit horizon;
- no comprehensive family/role cleanup was done in this phase.

The revision fixed the most concrete silent-effect, slot, caregiver-link, muse-token and factory-graduation failures. It did not turn Phase 17 into a higher-floor life simulator. Add no ninth pack until the conversion/relationship/horizon issues above are addressed.
